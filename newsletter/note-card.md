# Weekly note card

Fill this in once a week (5 minutes). Type numbers only where you see `=`. Leave a line blank if you don't have it.
A blank line is fine. The draft will leave that tile out. **Never guess a number.**

Where the numbers come from: MetroList → Prospector → **TrendVision** (or Market Statistics).
Set: Residential, single-family, one county at a time, the last full month.
How to read each number: see "How to Read Your Market Numbers" (PDF in the Drive folder).

## The line that must go with MLS numbers

When the numbers come from MetroList, the issue carries this line (the draft fills in the dates):

> Local numbers: MetroList® MLS, [date range], single-family homes, pulled [date]. Compared with [last month] and [same month last year]. Based on information from MetroList Services, Inc. Deemed reliable but not guaranteed.

If you don't fill in the card, the draft falls back to the county numbers in the
CALIFORNIA ASSOCIATION OF REALTORS® monthly report (median price, days on market, months of
inventory) and says so. New listings, pendings, and sale-to-list % only come from the MLS.
